How to Choose the Right Transfer Switch

1. Match to Your Generator

Your transfer switch amperage should match your generator’s output:

Generator SizeRecommended Switch
3,000-5,000W30 Amp
5,000-7,500W30 Amp
7,500-10,000W50 Amp
10,000W+50 Amp or higher

2. Count Your Critical Circuits

Most manual transfer switches offer 6-10 circuits. Make a list of what you must power:

  • Refrigerator
  • Freezer
  • Furnace/boiler
  • Well pump
  • Sump pump
  • Key lighting circuits
  • Medical equipment

3. Manual vs. Automatic

Choose Manual if:

  • You have a portable generator
  • You’re usually home during outages
  • Budget is a concern

Choose Automatic if:

  • You have a standby generator
  • You travel frequently
  • You need 24/7 protection

Important

All transfer switches should be installed by a licensed electrician. Improper installation can cause fires, electrocution, or damage to utility workers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best transfer switch for a portable generator?

The Reliance Controls 310CRK is our top pick for portable generators. It's a 30-amp, 10-circuit manual transfer switch that includes everything you need for installation.

Are automatic transfer switches worth the extra cost?

Yes, if you travel frequently or need protection when you're away. An ATS automatically starts your generator and transfers power within seconds of an outage.

What size transfer switch do I need?

Match your transfer switch to your generator's output and your electrical panel. Most homes use 30-amp or 50-amp switches for portable generators, or 100-200 amp switches for standby generators.

Can I install a transfer switch myself?

While some homeowners with electrical experience install manual transfer switches, we recommend hiring a licensed electrician. It's required by code in most areas and ensures safety.
