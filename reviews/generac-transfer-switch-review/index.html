Generac is the leading name in home backup power, and their transfer switches are designed to work seamlessly with their generator lineup. In this review, we’ll compare every Generac transfer switch to help you find the right fit.

Key Takeaways

  • Generac offers both automatic (for standby generators) and manual transfer switches
  • Automatic models work exclusively with Generac standby generators
  • Manual models like the Home Link work with any generator brand
  • Service-rated models can be installed before your main panel
  • All models meet UL and NEC requirements for safety

The RXSW200A3 is Generac’s flagship automatic transfer switch. As a service-rated unit, it installs between your utility meter and main electrical panel, allowing whole-house backup without circuit selection.

Why It’s Our Top Pick:

This switch handles up to 200 amps—enough for most residential electrical services. When utility power fails, it automatically signals your Generac generator to start, waits for stable power, and transfers your home within seconds. Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Generac transfer switches compatible with other generator brands?

Generac manual transfer switches work with any generator brand. However, Generac automatic transfer switches are designed specifically for Generac standby generators and may not be compatible with other brands.

How long do Generac transfer switches last?

Generac transfer switches are built to last 20-30 years with proper installation and maintenance. They come with a 5-year warranty for automatic models and 3-year warranty for manual models.

What's the difference between Generac RXSW and RTSC series?

The RXSW series are service-rated automatic transfer switches for whole-house backup. The RTSC series are load center transfer switches for specific circuits only.
