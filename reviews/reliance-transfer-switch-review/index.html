Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Reliance Controls a good brand?

Yes, Reliance Controls is one of the most trusted names in residential transfer switches. They've been manufacturing transfer switches for over 40 years and are known for quality, reliability, and excellent customer support.

What's included in a Reliance transfer switch kit?

Most Reliance kits include the transfer switch panel and installation hardware. The 'K' models (like 310CRK) include a complete kit with inlet box and power cord. The 'A' models (like 306A) include only the switch panel.

Can I install a Reliance transfer switch myself?

While Reliance switches come with detailed instructions, we recommend hiring a licensed electrician. Most jurisdictions require permits and professional installation for electrical work involving your main panel.
