The **RXSW200A3** is Generac's flagship automatic transfer switch. As a service-rated unit, it installs between your utility meter and main electrical panel, allowing whole-house backup without circuit selection.

**Why It's Our Top Pick:**

This switch handles up to 200 amps—enough for most residential electrical services. When utility power fails, it automatically signals your Generac generator to start, waits for stable power, and transfers your home within seconds. When utility power returns, it transfers back and shuts down the generator.

**Pros:**
- True whole-house backup
- Completely automatic operation
- Outdoor rated installation
- Works even when you're away
- Service entrance rated

**Cons:**
- Only works with Generac generators
- Requires standby generator purchase
- Higher cost
- Professional installation required

## Generac vs. Reliance: Which Should You Choose?

| Factor | Generac | Reliance |

| Price | Higher | Lower |

**Choose Generac if:** You have or plan to buy a Generac standby generator.

**Choose Reliance if:** You have a portable generator or want the best value.
