## How to Choose Your Reliance Model

### Step 1: Determine Your Amperage

| Generator Size | Recommended |
|----------------|-------------|
| 3,000-7,500W | 30 Amp |
| 7,500W+ | 50 Amp |

### Step 2: Count Your Circuits

Make a list of what you must power:
- Refrigerator ✓
- Furnace ✓
- Well pump ✓
- Sump pump ✓
- Lights (2-3 circuits) ✓
- Garage door ✓

If more than 6 items, get a 10-circuit model.

### Step 3: Kit or Switch Only?

- **First time?** Get a kit (CRK models)
- **Already have inlet?** Switch only (A models)

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip">
The kit models (like 310CRK) often cost only slightly more than buying components separately, and you're guaranteed everything is compatible.
</Callout>
