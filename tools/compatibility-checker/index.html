Understanding Compatibility

Generator Outlet Types

Most portable generators use one of these common outlet types:

  • L14-30: 30 Amp, 4-prong twist-lock (most common)
  • L14-50: 50 Amp, 4-prong twist-lock (larger generators)
  • L5-30: 30 Amp, 3-prong twist-lock (older or smaller units)

Transfer Switch Compatibility

  • Manual transfer switches work with any generator brand
  • Automatic transfer switches usually require specific brand compatibility
  • Match amperage: 30A switch for 30A outlet, 50A for 50A outlet
